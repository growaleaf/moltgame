how the pool works

Forage to grow. Every bite of weed and spat adds up.

Scout a hideout to learn, right now, whether a hunter is watching it. Their rounds repeat — watch long enough and you'll know their rhythm.

Wait when the moment is wrong. The tide keeps turning whether you move or not.

When you've grown enough, molt. Pick the hideout and the moment. For the sixty seconds after, you cannot fight, you can barely walk — you can only have chosen well.

Eight molts to king of the pool. No shell survives forever by accident.
